Install Briefing

Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer to install the bookmark.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar

    In Chrome or Edge, use this shortcut if the bar is hidden:

    Windows CtrlShiftB Mac ⌘ShiftB

  2. Drag this button to the bookmarks bar

    Briefing

    Hold the button, drag it up, and drop it next to your other bookmarks.

  3. Open Pike13 and click your new bookmark

    Sign in to your usual staff account first.

    The Music PlaceArts Educators Group

    Briefing opens in a new tab. Keep Pike13 open until the report controls appear, then choose a report and click Run.

Can't drag? Add the bookmark manually
  1. Bookmark any page, then right-click that bookmark and choose Edit.
  2. Name it Briefing and replace its entire URL with the code below.
  3. Save the bookmark, then click it while viewing Pike13.

If the new tab is blocked, allow pop-ups for your Pike13 site and try again. Use Re-run to refresh a report; after closing it, launch Briefing again from Pike13.
